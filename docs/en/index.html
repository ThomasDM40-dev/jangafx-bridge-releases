JangaFX Bridge — documentation

JangaFX Bridge is an add-on for Blender 4.5 and later. It links EmberGen (smoke, fire, sparks) or LiquiGen (liquids) with Blender:

  • you open your EmberGen (.ember) or LiquiGen (.liquigen) project directly in Blender;
  • the add-on automatically finds the exported files (VDB, Alembic), imports them and rebuilds the project's render: materials, lights, camera, ground, post-processing, frame rate and frame range;
  • it watches the exports folder: when you re-export from EmberGen, Blender updates;
  • it compares Blender's render to EmberGen's and can match the look on its own (Auto Match);
  • it exports the finished effect as a standalone .blend, to add to any other scene (for example sparks in a Formula 1 scene);
  • an AI assistant, running on your computer, can tweak the look on request.

Where to find the add-on in Blender

In the 3D viewport, press N to open the sidebar, then click the JangaFX tab (the JangaFX AI tab contains the AI assistant). Button labels are in English, like the rest of Blender; this documentation gives their exact name in bold.

The Documentation button, at the bottom of the Settings section, opens this page. In the help popups of the ? buttons, the Learn more link opens the right section directly.
